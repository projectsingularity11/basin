Lusion, Unseen Studio and Active Theory inform cinematic scale, spatial movement and typography; no proprietary assets or layouts were copied. Their earlier research remains in DESIGN_RESEARCH.md. The custom mark, ocean normal texture, pointer shader additions, underwater rays, and scene composition are original. The ocean is visual artwork, separately identified from the runoff model.

## Brand concepts

Three vector directions were considered: a contour-ring symbol, converging flow-line basin, and basin/B lockup. Selected basin/B: the outer curve acts as a collection boundary; the internal low-point line represents accumulated water. It retains a broad recognizable silhouette at favicon scale. See BRAND_CONCEPTS.svg and public/icon.svg. Syne supplies display geometry, Manrope readable controls; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for numeric values.

The user's existing `projectsingularity11/sakuga/components/water-surface.tsx` was also inspected through the authenticated GitHub API. Its capped eight-impulse, texture-displacement approach provides a concrete comparison: it uses an event-driven lightweight 2D pass, whereas BASIN embeds pointer disturbance in an already animated reflected ocean. Its private implementation was not copied into BASIN. A full reflected ocean has a materially different rendering cost; performance is measured separately rather than claiming Sakuga's frame rate carries over.
